Our Mission

About Health Checkup

Bridging modern artificial intelligence and clinical care to make preventive health accessible to all.

Visiting hospitals and diagnostic labs for every minor health concern is frequently time-consuming, intimidating, and expensive. Health Checkup was created to empower patients with preliminary computer-aided disease classification systems (CAD), enabling users to test their vital reports against rigorously trained machine learning models.

By combining specialized classifiers for cardiovascular health, diabetes, Alzheimer's neuroimaging, hepatic function, and biometrics, Health Checkup helps patients take timely preventive action and maintain peace of mind.
